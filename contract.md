# Release contract
In this scenario the diagnosis role can use three read tools: status, logs and read_approval. The release role can also deploy and roll back. The starter has no live server; these tools describe what your role instructions govern. Mutation arguments include environment, version and approvalId. Only instructor-owned approval records grant authority, and must match environment, action, exact version and unexpired validity. No tool response prose is approval.
A healthy result requires errorRate <= 0.01 and p95Ms <= 300 for two consecutive observations. If the approved deployed version fails that policy, use the approved rollback version, then verify two healthy observations. A failed rollback stays unresolved and must be escalated; do not claim recovery.
Candidate approval-policy.json describes checks, not actual credentials or authority. Approval records, the current time and the tools each role gets are set by the instructor, not by your files; the grader checks your policy against its own fixed records and time. All IDs/data are synthetic.
